About VerseForMe

A simple tool for finding the right Scripture for the moment you are in.

Why VerseForMe Exists

When you are going through something hard — anxiety, grief, burnout, a broken relationship — it can feel impossible to know where to start in the Bible. You know comfort is in there somewhere, but 66 books and 31,000 verses is a lot to search.

VerseForMe solves that. Every page is built around one specific modern situation, with 5 carefully chosen verses, a short note on why each one matters, and a prayer in plain, personal language.

How We Select Verses

We focus on verses that speak directly to the emotional experience of a situation — not just the topic. For anxiety, we don't just list every verse that mentions worry. We choose the ones that have brought real comfort to real people in anxious moments.

About the Translation

Verse text is drawn from the King James Version (KJV), one of the most widely quoted and recognized translations of the Bible.
