Finding Comfort After Pregnancy Loss

Experiencing a miscarriage can bring deep sorrow and confusion, leaving you feeling alone in your grief. These verses offer hope and comfort, reminding you that God understands your pain and is near.

Verses from Scripture

Psalm 34:18
“The Lord is close to the brokenhearted and saves those who are crushed in spirit.”
This verse reassures us that God is near during moments of deep sorrow and heartbreak.
Matthew 5:4
“Blessed are those who mourn, for they will be comforted.”
Jesus promises comfort to those who grieve, offering hope for healing in the midst of loss.
Jeremiah 29:11
“"For I know the plans I have for you," declares the Lord, "plans to prosper you and not to harm you, plans to give you hope and a future."”
God’s plans include hope and a future, even when current pain feels overwhelming.
Isaiah 41:10
“So do not fear, for I am with you; do not be dismayed, for I am your God. I will strengthen you and help you; I will uphold you with my righteous right hand.”
This verse reminds us that God offers strength and support in times of fear and sadness.
Revelation 21:4
“‘He will wipe every tear from their eyes. There will be no more death’ or mourning or crying or pain, for the old order of things has passed away."”
A promise of ultimate healing and the end of pain, offering hope beyond this life.

A Prayer for This Moment

God, in my sadness, please hold me close and remind me that I am not alone. Help me to find peace in your presence and trust in your plans for my future. Comfort my heart and heal my pain, one day at a time.
